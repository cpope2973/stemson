## About (1,705 characters)

Most organisations have invested heavily in technology. Far fewer have got the outcome they were promised. Adoption is patchy, the operating model hasn't changed, and the benefits in the business case never quite arrived.

The problem is rarely the technology. It's how the change is led, owned and adopted. Replace the system without fixing that, and the same problems come back on a new one.

Stemson turns technology investment into business outcomes that are adopted, owned and run well. We act as the leadership and the advisory voice: we lead the change, advise the C-suite, and leave a team that runs it without us. We start with ServiceNow, where our experience runs deepest, and apply the same approach to ERP, CRM, HCM and the rest of the enterprise estate.

We've sat in the chair. We've owned and run these programmes, carried the risk and answered to the board for the results. We hold ourselves to the same standard with clients.

How we work:
• Stemson Sounding: a fixed-fee transformation health check, under two weeks end to end. It tells you why the value isn't landing, and what to change first.
• Stemson Helm: a monthly advisory retainer for CEOs, CIOs and leadership teams.
• Stemson Refit: senior, hands-on leadership that lands the change: operating model, adoption and partner reset.

What you can hold us to:
• The senior people you meet are the people who do the work.
• One clear recommendation, not a menu of options.
• Outcomes agreed at the start and measured at the end: adoption, time to value, lower run cost, and benefits the board can see.

We work with CEOs, CIOs, COOs, CFOs, boards and private equity operating partners across the UK and Ireland, Europe and the GCC.
