## About (1,497 characters)

Most enterprise platforms cost more every year and deliver less. Upgrades get riskier, change gets slower, and eventually someone asks: "Should we replace it?"

Usually, the answer is no. The problem is rarely the technology. It's how the platform is owned, governed and changed. Replace it without fixing that, and you move the debt to a new system.

Stemson gets stalled platforms delivering again. We start with ServiceNow, where our experience runs deepest, and apply the same playbook to ERP, CRM and the rest of the enterprise estate.

We've sat in the chair. We've owned and run these platforms, carried the upgrade risk and answered to the board for the results. We hold ourselves to the same standard with clients.

How we work:
• Stemson Sounding: a fixed-fee, three-week diagnostic. It tells you whether you have a platform problem or an operating problem, and what to do about it.
• Stemson Helm: a monthly advisory retainer for CIOs and leadership teams.
• Stemson Refit: senior, hands-on leadership to land the change, including resetting your implementation partners.

What you can hold us to:
• The senior people you meet are the people who do the work.
• One clear recommendation, not a menu of options.
• Outcomes agreed at the start and measured at the end: lower run cost, routine upgrades, faster change, and value the board can see.

We work with CIOs, CTOs, COOs, CFOs, boards and private equity operating partners across the UK and Ireland, Europe and the GCC.
