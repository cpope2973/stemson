Giving back · Stemson Slipway

A million young people are waiting to be launched.

Nearly one in eight 16 to 24-year-olds in the UK is not in education, employment or training. They're known as NEETs. Entry-level roles are disappearing fastest in technology, the industry we work in, and AI is changing the first rung of the ladder faster than schools or employers can adapt. We've spent our careers building platforms and teams. We think the most important thing we can build is a way in.

Our programme

Stemson Slipway

A slipway is where a ship is built and launched. Slipway is our programme to help young people who are out of work, education or training take their first step into technology careers. It's built into how we run the firm, not added on afterwards.

Our principleEvery engagement we deliver should open a door for someone who's still waiting for theirs.
